Counterspace, 1957–2026: the weapons and the law that trailed them

Destructive anti-satellite testing has paused since November 2021. Jamming, spoofing, dazzling and cyber operations have not. Those non-kinetic methods are the only counterspace tools used in actual military operations. This page puts the technology and the legal response on one year axis.

How to read this page. On wide screens the law band stays pinned as you scroll. A shows kinetic tests by altitude, C non-kinetic operations, B how many states hold each capability, and The lag measures the years between a capability and the legal response. Items with a cube badge open a 3D scene. Each chart has a data table.

Low Earth orbit (to ~2,000 km) holds imaging satellites, the ISS and broadband constellations. GPS sits in medium Earth orbit (~20,200 km). Broadcast and communications satellites sit in the geostationary belt (~35,786 km). Every destructive intercept so far has been in LEO.

          B · Capability diffusion

          Number of states holding each counterspace capability, by decade, on a zero baseline. Solid bands mean demonstrated; hatched bands mean developing or latent. In the five-category view the stack counts state-capability pairs, so a state with two capabilities counts twice.

            The lag

            Years between a capability milestone and the legal or policy response that addressed it. An open ring means no binding rule has followed yet.
