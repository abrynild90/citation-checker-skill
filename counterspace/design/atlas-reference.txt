EDITORIAL IDEA
A scientific atlas: a strong reading spine, precise comparative graphics and
documentary scene plates. One dominant relationship per picture. The reader
should see weapons and law on the same year axis within about a minute, then
follow each observation to a source. Visual drama comes from composition,
scale and light, with explanatory qualifications beside the picture.
