## Changes and verification

Direct choosers expose all 13 scenes and 19 legal items. Compact introductions remain visible on phones; the complete camera menu replaces the clipped preset strip. Fitted phone views retain the important craft and aircraft. Chart annotations and country summaries fit; capability bars explicitly count memberships rather than distinct states. Source search, optional quiz reasoning and visible keys improve reading. Closed chart tooltips are inert.

Offline smoke and behavioral regression checks passed at 375, 900, 1024 and 1440 px, with no axe violations in tested states. Targeted phone scene checks passed 36 states for DN-2/SJ-21/GNSS and 42 proximity-episode states. All 13 scenes opened and closed in both visual review viewports.

## Remaining limits

Dense tests still benefit from zoom or the data table. The reference sections remain long because they preserve evidence and qualifications. Scene geometry is illustrative. This review does not independently validate historical or legal claims, certify full accessibility, or exhaustively test every physical device, assistive technology and camera/time combination.
