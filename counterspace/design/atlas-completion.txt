PROPOSED REVIEW RUBRIC — not the missing attachment's rubric
Rate hierarchy, chart clarity, scene explanation, motion, visual consistency,
responsive composition and accessibility from 0 to 4:
0 = inaccessible or misleading; 1 = major defects; 2 = material fixes needed;
3 = strong with identified minor defects; 4 = no material defects in reviewed
samples. Each rating needs evidence paths, viewport/theme/state and findings.
Missing evidence is unreviewed, not zero or a pass. Do not publish an aggregate
score that hides blockers. No score has been assigned in this session.

Source inspection found a hypothesis for the animation-start report:
src/scenes/gl-host.js play() exits its frame loop when document.hidden is true,
without updating the playing flag. src/scene-ui.js interrupts on a later hidden
visibility event, but first-open while already hidden needs reproduction. Test
visible first-open, hidden first-open then foreground, background/return,
reduced motion, diagram fallback, replay and immediate user interruption before
choosing a fix. This is a hypothesis, not a confirmed diagnosis.
