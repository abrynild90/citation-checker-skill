Counterspace, 1957–2026: the weapons and the law that trailed them

Destructive anti-satellite testing has paused since November 2021. Jamming, spoofing, dazzling and cyber operations have not. Those non-kinetic methods are the only counterspace tools used in actual military operations. This page puts the technology and the legal response on one year axis.

Low Earth orbit (to ~2,000 km) holds imaging satellites, the ISS and broadband constellations. GPS sits in medium Earth orbit (~20,200 km). Broadcast and communications satellites sit in the geostationary belt (~35,786 km). Every destructive intercept so far has been in LEO.
